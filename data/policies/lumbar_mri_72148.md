# Prior Authorization Policy: MRI Lumbar Spine without Contrast (CPT 72148)

## Policy Overview

Prior authorization is required for MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast
(CPT 72148) for non-emergent indications. All three criteria below must be
met for approval. Red-flag findings (see C3) permit immediate imaging without
prior authorization.

## Required Criteria

### C1: Duration of low back pain symptoms

Patient must have documented low back pain symptoms for a minimum of 6
consecutive weeks. Documentation must include symptom onset date or a
clinical note confirming symptom duration of at least 6 weeks.

### C2: Failure of conservative management

Patient must have documented failure of a course of conservative management,
including physician-supervised physical therapy administered for a minimum
of 6 weeks. Documentation must include the treating provider, treatment dates,
and clinical outcome demonstrating lack of adequate improvement.

### C3: Absence of red-flag findings

The ordering clinician must document the absence of all of the following
red-flag findings at the time of the request:
- New or progressive neurological deficit (motor weakness or sensory loss
  in a dermatomal distribution)
- Bowel or bladder dysfunction
- Saddle anesthesia
- Fever of unknown origin
- Unexplained significant weight loss (> 10 lbs in 3 months)

If any red-flag finding is present, MRI is immediately indicated and prior
authorization is not required.

## Documentation Requirements

- Clinical note documenting symptom onset date or confirmed duration >= 6 weeks
- Physical therapy records or primary care notes with treatment start/end dates
  and outcome documentation
- Clinical examination note explicitly documenting absence of red-flag findings
