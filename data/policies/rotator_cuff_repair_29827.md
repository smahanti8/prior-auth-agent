# Medical Policy: Rotator Cuff Repair (CPT 29827)

Effective: 2026-01-01 · Payer: Acme Health Plan · Policy ID: SURG-041

## Prior Authorization Criteria

Arthroscopic rotator cuff repair (CPT 29827) is considered medically necessary
when ALL of the following criteria are met:

1. Full-thickness rotator cuff tear confirmed by MRI or MR arthrogram of the
   affected shoulder.

2. Failure of at least 3 months of conservative management, which must include
   physician-supervised physical therapy. Documentation must include treating
   provider, treatment dates, and clinical outcome demonstrating lack of adequate
   improvement.

3. Documented functional impairment affecting activities of daily living, as
   evidenced by a formal functional assessment, shoulder disability questionnaire,
   or equivalent standardized instrument with scoring.

## Exclusions

- Partial-thickness tears not meeting full-thickness criteria on imaging.
- Conservative management period of less than 3 months unless the tear is
  acute traumatic in nature.

## Documentation Requirements

Clinical notes must include MRI report confirming full-thickness tear, physical
therapy records with start and end dates and outcome note, and a functional
assessment score or equivalent objective functional documentation.
