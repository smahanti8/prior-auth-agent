# Prior Authorization Policy: Laparoscopic Sleeve Gastrectomy (CPT 43644)

## Policy Overview

Prior authorization is required for laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy
(CPT 43644). All three criteria below must be met for approval.

## Required Criteria

### C1: BMI threshold

Patient must have a documented body mass index (BMI) of 40 kg/m2 or greater,
OR a documented BMI of 35 kg/m2 or greater with at least one obesity-related
comorbidity. Qualifying comorbidities include: Type 2 diabetes mellitus,
hypertension, obstructive sleep apnea, or clinically significant
cardiovascular disease.

### C2: Physician-supervised diet program

Patient must have documented completion of a physician-supervised, structured
diet and lifestyle modification program for a minimum of 6 consecutive months.
The program must be supervised by a licensed physician or registered dietitian
operating under physician oversight. Self-directed weight loss attempts do not
satisfy this requirement. Documentation must include program start and end
dates, attendance or visit records, and a clinical summary from the supervising
provider.

### C3: Psychiatric or psychological evaluation

Patient must have completed a formal psychiatric or psychological evaluation
within 12 months prior to the authorization request. The evaluation must
include documented clearance for bariatric surgery, confirming the absence
of active psychiatric contraindications.

## Documentation Requirements

- Current BMI with supporting weight measurements (dated within 90 days)
- Documentation of qualifying comorbidities if BMI is 35–39.9 kg/m2
- Records from the supervised diet program: provider name, program dates,
  visit summary, and clinical outcome note
- Psychiatric or psychological evaluation report with explicit clearance
  statement for bariatric surgery
