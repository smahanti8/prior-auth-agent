# Prior Authorization Policy: Total Knee Arthroplasty (CPT 27447)

## Policy Overview

Prior authorization is required for total knee arthroplasty (CPT 27447).
All three criteria below must be met for approval.

## Required Criteria

### C1: Diagnosis of severe knee osteoarthritis

Patient must have a documented diagnosis of severe knee osteoarthritis,
Kellgren-Lawrence Grade III or IV, confirmed on standing weight-bearing
radiographs of the affected knee.

### C2: Failure of conservative management for at least 6 months

Patient must have documented failure of conservative management for a minimum
of 6 consecutive months, including physician-supervised physical therapy and
anti-inflammatory medication. Documentation must specify treatment duration
and clinical outcome.

### C3: BMI less than 40 at time of surgical evaluation

Patient body mass index (BMI) must be documented as less than 40 kg/m2 at
the time of the pre-surgical evaluation. Morbid obesity (BMI >= 40) is a
relative contraindication and requires case-by-case review.

## Documentation Requirements

- Standing AP weight-bearing radiographs with Kellgren-Lawrence grade
  documented by the interpreting radiologist or ordering physician
- Physical therapy records or primary care notes documenting treatment
  start date, duration, and clinical response
- BMI measurement dated within 90 days of the authorization request
